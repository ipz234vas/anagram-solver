# Privacy Policy for Anagram Solver

**Last Updated: May 18, 2026**

## 1. Introduction
This Privacy Policy governs the data processing practices of Anagram Solver (the "Application"), a web-based puzzle application built using React and Vite. This Application operates as a purely client-side software solution, meaning all processing of data occurs directly within the user's web browser.

## 2. Compliance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)
The Application is designed with privacy by design principles to ensure full alignment with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) for users residing within the European Economic Area (EEA).

### 2.1. Data Controller
Since the Application operates locally on your device and does not transmit data to an external server, you act as the sole controller of your data. The developer does not collect, store, access, or process any of your personal data.

### 2.2. Legal Basis for Processing
The processing of local operational data is based on your explicit actions and consent (Article 6(1)(a) of the GDPR) when registering a local profile or playing the game, necessary for the technical performance and core functionality of the Application.

## 3. Types of Data Processed
The Application utilizes Web Storage APIs (specifically LocalStorage) to store the following information:
- **Authentication Data:** Locally hashed usernames and credentials created for profile management.
- **Gameplay Statistics:** Game scores, streak counters, win rates, and total playtime metrics.
- **Application Configuration:** User preferences including difficulty level, category filters, and timer settings.

## 4. No Server-Side Storage or Third-Party Sharing
- **Zero Server Transmission:** No personal data or gameplay telemetry is transmitted to any remote servers or external databases. 
- **No Third-Party Analytics:** The Application does not integrate third-party tracking scripts, advertising SDKs, or external analytics providers.
- **No Data Sharing:** As no data is collected, no information is shared, sold, or distributed to third parties.

## 5. Data Retention and Deletion
All data remains stored in your browser's persistent storage until explicitly cleared. 

### 5.1. Your Rights under GDPR
Under the GDPR, you retain full rights over your data, including the right to access, rectify, restrict, or erase your records. 

### 5.2. Exercising Your Rights
Because all information resides strictly within your browser environment, you can exercise your right to erasure (the "right to be forgotten") at any time by performing one of the following actions:
- Clearing your web browser cache and local storage data for this Application's origin.
- Deleting your user profile using internal Application settings, if provided.

## 6. Security
Data security is dependent on the security protocols established by your web browser and operating system. Users are advised to secure their devices and browser environments to protect their local data storage from unauthorized access.

## 7. Amendments to This Policy
The author reserves the right to modify this Privacy Policy at any time. Any changes will be made available by updating the version and effective date located at the top of this document within the repository.

## 8. Contact Information
For any inquiries regarding this privacy framework, you may open an issue in the official project repository or contact Andrii Volynets.
